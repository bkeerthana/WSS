LAB EXERCISES: DOM-BASED XSS

Exercise 1:
-----------
Open vulnerable.html and enter plain text.
Observe how the value appears in the preview area.

Exercise 2:
-----------
Enter this payload:
<img src=x onerror=alert('DOM XSS')>
Question:
Why does the alert execute?

Exercise 3:
-----------
Check the browser URL after clicking Update.
Question:
Where is the payload stored?

Exercise 4:
-----------
Identify the vulnerable line in JavaScript.
Task:
Explain why innerHTML is dangerous with untrusted data.

Exercise 5:
-----------
Open secure.html and enter the same payload.
Question:
Why is the payload displayed as text instead of executing?

Exercise 6:
-----------
Compare these two lines:
output.innerHTML = value;
output.textContent = value;
Question:
What is the difference?

Exercise 7:
-----------
Modify vulnerable.html and replace innerHTML with textContent.
Test again.

Exercise 8:
-----------
Explain the difference between:
- reflected XSS
- stored XSS
- DOM-based XSS

Exercise 9:
-----------
Create a crafted URL containing the payload and share it in the lab.
Question:
Why can DOM-based XSS be delivered through a link?

Exercise 10:
------------
Write a short report with:
- source of input
- sink used
- effect
- mitigation
